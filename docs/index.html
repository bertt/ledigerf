Ledig Erf, Utrecht concept design: before / after

Utrecht is turning the car-dominated Ledig Erf, the Catharijnesingel and the Bleekstraat into a greener, people-first southern gate to the city centre. Concept version October 2026 – not yet adopted.

From traffic junction to green city square

Ledig Erf is where the Oudegracht, Singel, Vaartsche Rijn and Kromme Rijn meet – once the southern gate of Utrecht, now a car-dominated junction. The concept design makes it a place to walk, cycle, sit and meet.

🚫

Car-free

Vaartscherijnbrug closed to motorised traffic; emergency services keep access.

🧱

One square

Same paving between the four bridges, matching the Tolsteegbarrière.

🚶

Walking

Routes along the water and across the square, step-free for wheelchairs and prams.

🚲

Cycling

Red-asphalt route and 40–60 new bike parking spaces at the edges.

🌳

Green

20–30 new trees, 1,500–2,000 m² of paving replaced by green; plane trees stay.

💧

Climate

Lowered green areas collect rainwater and reduce flooding and heat.

🏛️

History

Tram house, historic bridges and “De Tourrenners” get room to be seen.

🚌

Bus line 2

Still under study: the plan shows a version with and without the bus.

The plan also covers the Catharijnesingel (green cycle and walking route along the canal) and the Bleekstraat (a greener street). This is a concept sketch design; exact tree numbers and positions follow in the next phase.
